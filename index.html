Questions builders ask

HackPulse, Explained Clearly

What is HackPulse?

HackPulse is an engineering-first hackathon blog and builder journal featuring winning project postmortems, architecture diagrams, pitch breakdowns, and copyable sprint templates.

Can anyone publish a hackathon story?

Yes. Whether you placed 1st overall or learned a tough lesson at Hour 36, you can draft your build log right in the Author Studio or pitch our editorial team via the form below.

Are starter snippets allowed at MLH or ETHGlobal?

Yes. Our boilerplate patterns and SDK helpers follow standard open-source MIT rules, helping you eliminate setup overhead while writing all core product logic during the event.

How are winning teardowns verified?

Every featured postmortem includes links to public demo tracks, latency benchmarks, and real architecture trade-offs reviewed by past hackathon judges and senior engineers.

Do I need an account to bookmark or read?

No. Reading all field notes, copying code blocks, bookmarking stories locally, and testing the interactive Author Studio work immediately in your browser.

Can organizations host a hackathon series here?

Yes. University chapters, developer tools companies, and global sponsors partner with HackPulse to publish bounty walkthroughs and official winner spotlights.
