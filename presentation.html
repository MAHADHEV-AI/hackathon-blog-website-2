Slide 02 • Visual Engineering

Design Philosophy & Aesthetic Translation

Strictly recreating the deep obsidian atmosphere, 8px geometry, and sweeping light kinetics of modern SaaS platforms.

Obsidian Void Canvas

Base background at #030712 with layered electric blue (#2563eb) and cyan (#06b6d4) radial mesh gradients.

8px Glass Design System

Unified 8px corner radii, frosted glass surfaces (backdrop-filter: blur(22px)), and crisp 1px translucent borders.

Hero Orbit Centerpiece

3D floating card featuring 4 rotating sprint chips, a continuous light sheen animation, and pulsing live telemetry stream.
